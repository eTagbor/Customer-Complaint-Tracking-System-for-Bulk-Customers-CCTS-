# Customer-Complaint-Tracking-System-for-Bulk-Customers-CCTS-
This repository is for CCTS, a system that automatically receives, records, assigns, follows up and closes bulk-customer complaints on WhatsApp and a web form, so no complaint is lost or forgotten.
Many businesses serve customers who purchase products in large quantities. When these bulk customers raise complaints about their orders, the complaints are often handled through WhatsApp messages, phone calls, notebooks, or verbal communication. Because of this, the business frequently struggles to answer basic questions about any given complaint:
Which customer made the complaint
What the complaint was about
When the complaint was made
Whether someone is currently working on it
Whether the complaint has been resolved
As a result, complaints can be forgotten, delayed, duplicated, or poorly handled leading to customer dissatisfaction and, in some cases, the loss of important bulk-buying customers.
The Customer Complaint Tracker for Bulk Buyers is an information management system designed to help businesses efficiently record, monitor, assign, and resolve complaints received from customers who make bulk purchases. The system will provide a centralized platform where customer information, purchase details, complaints, assigned staff, follow-up activities, and resolution status can be recorded and monitored. This will reduce the dependence on WhatsApp messages, phone calls, notebooks, and verbal communication for complaint management.
